Your photos stay on your hard drive

Cloud photo apps copy your library to company servers. A breach there can expose many people's photos at once, and the company keeps the data. QuackSort keeps the library on your own computer, so there is no upload and no account.

    What does go online, and only when you choose

    • Guest enrollment (optional): a guest registers on the QuackSort website with your event code. Their name and selfie are stored online only until your app syncs them, and are removed from the cloud within a day after syncing. Your event photo library is never part of this.
    • Sharing: QuackShare sends a peer-to-peer link from your computer, and Google Drive uploads to the recipient's own Drive. Either way, only the photos you pick.
    • Face matching and sorting always run on your computer.

    Local storage is not magic. Turn on disk encryption and keep a backup in case the computer is lost.

    Questions people ask

    Does it need the internet?

    Sorting and album creation run on your computer, so they work offline after setup. You only need internet to sync guest enrollments and to share photos.

    Where do my photos go?

    They stay on your computer. They only leave it when you choose to share them, using QuackShare (a peer-to-peer link) or Google Drive.

    What if a guest never registers?

    Faces that are not enrolled stay unnamed. You can label them yourself afterward.

    Can one photo show up for several people?

    Yes. A group photo is copied into the folder of every enrolled person found in it.
