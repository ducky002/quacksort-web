Before you download QuackSort

QuackSort is offered without a purchase requirement. Access is approved according to your intended use case, so downloading the app does not automatically activate it.

Why Device Activation is required

We use Device Activation to make QuackSort available thoughtfully and help prevent unauthorized redistribution of activated access. This gives us an opportunity to understand your intended use and provide access appropriate to it while keeping the app available without a paywall.

The app creates an activation request code (shown as Machine ID in the app) by hashing selected device identifiers locally. You send us this code with a brief description of your intended use, and, once approved, receive a digitally signed activation code linked to that device. The app checks the code locally to confirm that it was issued by QuackSort and matches your device. Any access period associated with the code also applies.

What activation involves

Activation does not require you to send us your photos, photo library, or face data. The activation code does not give us remote access to your computer or its files.

When you request access by email, we receive your activation request code, email address, and whatever information you include in your request. Although the activation request code is a hash rather than a list of raw hardware identifiers, it still identifies your installation for activation purposes. Please do not include photos or other sensitive material in your activation request.

This activation process is separate from QuackSort's photo features. Cloud enrollment, synchronization, and sharing features involve transferring the information needed for those features to the relevant services or recipients. Device Activation itself does not require uploading your photo library.

Request access

  1. Download and open QuackSort to view your activation request code.
  2. Email quacksort123@gmail.com with the subject Authorization Code, your activation request code, and a brief, one-line description of how you intend to use QuackSort. We ask for this description to understand your needs and help improve the app.
  3. Once your request is approved, enter the activation code supplied in our reply.

If you change computers or make significant hardware or system changes, you may need a new activation code. Contact us with your updated activation request code so we can review your access.

Thank you for helping us make QuackSort available responsibly.
